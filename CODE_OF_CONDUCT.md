# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

This learning community welcomes different experience levels, backgrounds,
identities, and perspectives. Participate with patience, respect, and an
interest in helping others learn.

## Expected behavior

- Offer constructive technical feedback and explain your reasoning.
- Ask for clarification without belittling learners.
- Credit others' work and respect copyright/privacy.
- Accept corrections and keep discussions relevant.
- Share sanitized reproductions rather than private operational information.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disruption,
and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Scope and moderation

These expectations apply to issues, discussions, pull requests, and other
spaces representing this project. Maintainers may remove content, close
discussions, or restrict participation to protect the community.
Responses should be proportionate and consider context.

## Reporting

Contact the repository owner privately through an available contact method
on their GitHub profile. Do not disclose sensitive details or identify
affected people in a public issue. If private contact is unavailable, ask
publicly only for a private reporting channel, without describing the incident.
For immediate safety concerns use appropriate local authorities or GitHub's
reporting tools.
